# Lab documentation template

Copy this outline into `<Provider>/<Lab-Name>/README.md` when adding a lab. Replace the prompts with verified details; remove sections that do not apply.

## Overview

State the goal, course or training context, and date. Identify whether the lab was guided and which resources were provided.

## Environment and services

List the services and tools actually used. Do not publish account identifiers, credentials, connection strings, or sensitive resource details.

## What I configured

Describe completed steps, separating work you performed from preconfigured resources.

## Results and evidence

Save reviewed screenshots under `screenshots/` and use relative links with meaningful alt text. Add `docs/evidence.md` if an evidence inventory is useful.

## Skills practiced

Tie each skill to a concrete configuration or result. Describe learning without implying production ownership or unsupported outcomes.

## Limitations and next steps

Identify incomplete work, remaining questions, and follow-up practice. Label planned work clearly.

## Cleanup

Record cleanup performed or the training environment's lifecycle, when known. Do not claim resources were removed without evidence.

## Before publishing

Review text, image metadata, and screenshots for secrets, account IDs, personal information, and course material restrictions. Check every local link and image reference.
